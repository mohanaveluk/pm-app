Material Requisition review and approval flow

How a requisition moves from draft to approval, who acts at each step, which emails go out, and what to check while testing. Statuses below are exactly what the list and the status chips show.

Version 3 · 23 Oct 2026 · includes the approver edit option

What changed
  • Approvers can now edit the requisition from the approval page while it is Reviewed, before they decide.
  • An approver who edited must explain the changes in their decision comment.
  • Each person can edit only until they record their decision. Once Approved, nobody can edit it.
  • While Reviewed, the requester can no longer edit; only the approvers whose decision is due.

Who does what

Reviewers and approvers are internal, active users picked on the Header step. Any one of them settles their round.

Requester

  • Creates, saves as draft, deletes a draft.
  • Submits with at least 1 reviewer and 1 approver.
  • Edits and resubmits a Returned requisition.
  • Changes reviewers until it is Reviewed, approvers until it is Approved.

Screens: /material-requisitions, Create, Edit

Reviewer

  • Acts only while Under Review.
  • Decides: Reviewed, Return, Reject, Cancel.
  • Can edit the requisition during review; a comment is then required.
  • One decision per round.

Screens: /material-requisitions/reviews

Approver

  • Acts only while Reviewed.
  • Decides: Approved, Return, Reject, Cancel.
  • Sees the reviewers' comments in the approval page and email.
  • New: can edit the requisition before deciding (Edit Requisition on the approval page); a comment is then required.
  • One decision per round.

Screens: /material-requisitions/approvals

Administrator

  • Hold, release, disable, re-enable, mark obsolete.
  • Edits reviewers and approvers from the list menu.
  • Maps users to projects; requesters only see mapped projects.

Screens: list More Actions, /admin/user-project-mappings

Rules to verify

  • Submit needs at least one reviewer, one approver, a WBS, a Required on Site date and at least one material line.
  • Any one reviewer's decision settles the review; the others no longer need to act.
  • Any one approver's decision settles the approval.
  • Return, Reject and Cancel need a comment. Reviewed and Approved don't, unless that reviewer or approver edited the requisition.
  • Reviewers can be changed until the requisition is Reviewed; approvers until it is Approved.
  • Only active internal users can be picked as reviewer or approver.
  • A reviewer acts only while Under Review; an approver only while Reviewed. Acting twice in a round is refused.
  • Resubmitting starts a new round: earlier decisions are cleared and the round number goes up.
  • Only a draft can be deleted.
  • Reviewers can edit until they review; approvers until they approve. Once Approved, nobody can edit it.
  • Once a purchase order is raised, the requisition is locked: no edits or decisions. Its project, client and WBS can't be edited either.
  • The project dropdown only lists projects the requester is mapped to (admins see all).
  • Every notification has an "Open Related Record" link back to the requisition.

Test checklist

Use three accounts: a requester, two reviewers and two approvers. Ticks are kept in this browser only.
